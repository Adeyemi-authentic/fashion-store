# Returns and refunds

FlyFit is a fictional brand and this is a concept store: nothing is really sold. This page describes how returns would work, so the shop and its assistant have real rules to follow.

## Return window

You can return most items within 14 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be unworn, unwashed and have their tags attached.

## Items that can't be returned

- Gele, head wraps and other headwear, for hygiene reasons, unless they arrive faulty.
- Bridal sets, once the 14 days have passed.
- Anything that has been altered, worn or washed.

## Exchanging for another size

Size exchanges are free, within the same 14 days. If the size you want is sold out, you get a full refund instead.

## How to start a return

Email returns@flyfit.example with your order reference and the item you are returning. We reply within one working day with a free pickup slot (Lagos) or a prepaid courier label (rest of Nigeria).

## Refunds

Refunds go back to the card or bank account you paid with, through Paystack. They are sent within 2 working days of the item reaching us; your bank may take 5 to 10 more working days to show the money.

## Faulty or wrong items

If an item arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered, tell us within 7 days of delivery. We collect it for free and send a replacement, or refund you if there's none left.

## Payments taken after an order expired

If you pay after your 15-minute hold has ended and the item has sold out in the meantime, you are refunded in full automatically and get an email saying so.
