# Shipping and delivery

FlyFit is a fictional brand and this is a concept store: nothing is really shipped. This page describes how delivery would work, so the shop and its assistant have real rules to follow.

## Delivery cost

Delivery is free on every order, anywhere in Nigeria. There is no minimum order and no extra charge at checkout: the total you pay is the total of the items.

## Delivery times

- Lagos: 1 to 2 working days after payment.
- Abuja, Port Harcourt and Ibadan: 2 to 4 working days.
- Everywhere else in Nigeria: 3 to 7 working days.

Orders paid before 12 noon (Lagos time) on a working day leave the studio the same day. Orders paid later, or at a weekend or public holiday, leave on the next working day.

## Tracking an order

Your order confirmation email has your order reference. Signed-in customers can see every order and its status on the Account page, or ask the shopping assistant. An order goes from "paid" to "fulfilled" when it leaves the studio.

## International delivery

We only deliver within Nigeria for now.

## Unpaid orders

Items in an unpaid order are held for you for 15 minutes. If payment doesn't arrive in time, the hold ends and the items go back on sale.
